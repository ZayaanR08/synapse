SYNAPSE: put it on your iPhone home screen
==========================================

These files are the whole app. To get a link you can open on your phone,
host them for free on GitHub Pages:

1. Make a free account at github.com (skip if you have one).
2. Click New repository. Name it synapse, set it to Public, and create it.
3. Click "uploading an existing file" and drag in EVERYTHING from this folder
   (index.html, manifest.webmanifest, sw.js and the .png icons). Commit.
4. Go to Settings > Pages. Under Branch pick "main" and "/ (root)", then Save.
5. Wait a minute. Your app lives at  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/synapse/

On your iPhone: open that link in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
It gets its own icon, opens full-screen, works offline, and keeps your scores.

To update the app later, upload the new files to the same repository.
